How the exchange works

1. The organizer creates a group and shares its code.
2. Everyone joins and submits exactly one wish.
3. The organizer checks the submission list.
4. Once everyone has submitted, the organizer starts the draw.
5. Each participant sees only the gift item assigned to them — never the wish owner's name.
